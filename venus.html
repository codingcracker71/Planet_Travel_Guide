What is Venus?

Welcome to Venus: Earth's twin planet! Venus is the brightest and hottest planet due to its thick atmosphere, which traps a lot of heat! A day on Venus is 243 Earth days while a year is 225 Earth days. Due to its small axial tilt, there is no change in the seasons.Scientists have utilized the streaks on Venus' clouds to further absorb the UV radiation to prevent the planet from getting hotter. Efforts are still being made to find a water source on the planet, so the current water source is imports from Earth made by the Space Water Delivery Service.
